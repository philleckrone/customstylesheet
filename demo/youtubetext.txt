Custom Stylesheet Editor is a Paratext tool for a project's custom.sty, the file that changes how markers look and behave in that project. Change a stock marker, for example its bold, italic or color, define new markers, and remove markers you don't want, with each stock value shown beside your own. Only the lines you change are written to custom.sty.

Download Install_Phils_tools.exe from the Paratext Plugins folder, and tick Custom Stylesheet Editor in it:
